FOR CUSTOMERS

From inspiration
to your environment.

  1. Browse for the outcome you want.
  2. Try a synthetic-data demo. Some run locally.
  3. Fork the repo, or use its template option if available.
  4. Deploy with the README steps in your own Snowflake account.
  5. Adapt with Cortex Code: your data, your workflows, your guardrails.

Review prerequisites, permissions, compute costs, and security before connecting real data. These examples do not establish production readiness.
